How should the baseline fill behave?

The fill should be relative to a reference Y value (e.g. threshold = 133W). Which visual style fits your use case?

A — Bidirectional, two colors

Fill green above the baseline, red below. Shows surplus vs. deficit at a glance. Best for threshold comparisons.

B — Bidirectional, one color

Fill the region between series and baseline with the series color (both above and below). Simpler — just shows deviation from baseline.

C — One-sided only

Fill only when the series is above (or below) the baseline. Below-threshold region is unfilled. Config controls which side.
